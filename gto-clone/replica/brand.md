## Palette
Dark neutrals plus a teal accent (`--accent #3fb8a4`), all in `replica/design/tokens.json`. Action colours follow the common solver convention (aggressive = warm reds, call = green, fold = blue); that convention isn't ownable. The original's exact brand hex wasn't measured (recon was text-only), so `brand.json` `colors` is empty: **to run** with a colour picker before any public launch. Contrast: 0 AA failures.

## Logo brief
- Idea: four rounded squares in a 2×2, a tiny piece of a range grid, coloured raise, call, fold and accent. "A spot, read at a glance."
- Mark: symbol + wordmark (Inter 700). The symbol works alone at 16px (already the favicon).
- Deliverables: SVG, 1024×1024 opaque app icon, favicon set, 1200×630 social image.
- Must not resemble the original's mark: no hat, star, wand or wizard imagery.

## Voice: plain, encouraging, exact
- Plain, not dumbed down: say "you need 33% to call", not "MDF-adjusted threshold".
- Encouraging, not cheesy: "Best move", never "Crushing it!!".
- Exact, not cold: numbers always come with what they mean.

Do / don't: explain the why / don't just show a red X. Name the hand ("top pair with a flush draw") / don't show a combo code alone. Admit limits ("demo strategies") / don't call heuristics "GTO". Short labels / no jargon in buttons. Second person / no "the user".

Key strings (already in the app, all written fresh): "Computing strategy…", "Hover or click a hand in the grid.", "Deal the flop", "Pick a drill, play the spot, and see how your choice compares with the strategy.", "Make a decision to see how it compares with the strategy.", "Waiting for the other players…", "No sessions yet: play a few hands in Practice…", "Not in this player's range at this point.", "You chose … · best was …", "Strategies here are generated by simple heuristics for practice. They are not solver output."
